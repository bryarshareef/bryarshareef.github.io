      <h1 id="faculty-name">Bryar Shareef, Ph.D.</h1>
      <p class="faculty-profile__title">Multimodal and agentic AI for medicine, scientific discovery, and spatial intelligence</p>
      <p class="faculty-profile__summary">
        I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at UNLV and director of the Advanced AI Research Lab. My research develops reliable artificial intelligence that connects medical images, language, biomedical data, and human expertise.
      </p>
      <p class="faculty-profile__summary">
        Medical and biomedical AI form the foundation of this work. From that foundation, my group studies multimodal learning, model reliability, scientific discovery, and intelligent systems that can reason in visual and spatial environments.
      </p>

      <div>
        <p class="faculty-overline">Research</p>
        <h2 id="research-heading">Connected research areas</h2>
      </div>
      <p>Our work begins with healthcare and biomedical challenges, then extends to the methods and intelligent systems needed to address them responsibly.</p>
    </header>

    <div class="faculty-research-grid">
      <article>
        <h3>Multimodal and vision&ndash;language AI</h3>
        <p>Learning from images, text, prompts, and structured clinical information to build grounded and useful AI systems.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>Medical image analysis</h3>
        <p>Robust analysis of ultrasound, MRI, histopathology, and 3D biomedical images for clinically meaningful applications.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>Reliable and interpretable AI</h3>
        <p>Reliability, generalization, uncertainty, explanation, and careful evaluation with experts kept in the decision process.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>EHR and clinical data intelligence</h3>
        <p>Electronic health records, clinical time series, and structured data for early warning and decision support.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>3D vision understanding and world models</h3>
        <p>Representing and reasoning about objects, scenes, motion, and interactions in three-dimensional environments.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>Agentic AI, spatial intelligence, and AR/VR</h3>
        <p>Intelligent agents and immersive systems that reason and act in visual and spatial environments.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>AI for scientific and biomedical discovery</h3>
        <p>Computational pathology, bioinformatics, image&ndash;omics integration, and spatial transcriptomics.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>AI for drug discovery</h3>
        <p>Molecular representation, graph learning, candidate prioritization, and data-driven biomedical discovery.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>AI for forensic analysis</h3>
        <p>Computational analysis of craniofacial, dental, imaging, and other multimodal forensic evidence.</p>
      </article>
    </div>
