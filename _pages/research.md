  <p>Medical and biomedical AI form the foundation of our research. We develop reliable learning systems for multimodal data, scientific discovery, clinical decision support, and visual and spatial reasoning.</p>
</header>

<div class="research-program-grid">
  <section class="research-program-card">

    <div>
      <p>We study models that connect images, language, prompts, and structured information for grounded analysis, reporting, and decision support.</p>
      <ul class="tag-list" aria-label="Multimodal AI research topics">
        <li>Vision&ndash;language models</li><li>Image&ndash;text learning</li><li>Visual grounding</li><li>Prompt-guided analysis</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="research-program-card">
    <h2>Medical and biomedical image analysis</h2>
    <div>
      <p>We develop methods for diagnosis, segmentation, risk assessment, and structured reporting across 2D and 3D biomedical imaging.</p>
      <ul class="tag-list" aria-label="Medical imaging research topics">
        <li>Ultrasound</li><li>Brain MRI</li><li>Histopathology</li><li>Dental and craniofacial CBCT</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="research-program-card">
    <h2>Reliable and interpretable AI</h2>
    <div>
      <p>We investigate model robustness, generalization, uncertainty, explanation, and evaluation methods that keep domain experts meaningfully involved.</p>
      <ul class="tag-list" aria-label="Reliable AI research topics">
        <li>Model reliability</li><li>Interpretability</li><li>Uncertainty</li><li>Human-in-the-loop evaluation</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="research-program-card">

    <div>
      <p>We analyze electronic health records, clinical time series, laboratory measurements, and other structured data for early warning, risk prediction, and clinical decision support.</p>
      <ul class="tag-list" aria-label="EHR and clinical data research topics">
        <li>Electronic health records</li><li>Clinical time series</li><li>Risk prediction</li><li>Decision support</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="research-program-card">
    <h2>3D vision understanding and world models</h2>
    <div>
      <p>We study how AI systems represent, understand, and reason about objects, scenes, motion, and interactions in three-dimensional environments.</p>
      <ul class="tag-list" aria-label="3D vision and world-model research topics">
        <li>3D vision</li><li>Scene understanding</li><li>World models</li><li>Visual and spatial reasoning</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="research-program-card">
    <h2>Agentic AI, spatial intelligence, and AR/VR</h2>
    <div>
      <p>We explore agents that reason and act in visual and spatial environments, including synthetic 3D worlds and immersive systems for healthcare, education, and workforce training.</p>
      <ul class="tag-list" aria-label="Agentic and immersive AI research topics">

      <ul class="tag-list" aria-label="Scientific and biomedical discovery research topics">
